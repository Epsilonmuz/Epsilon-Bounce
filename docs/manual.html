Epsilon Bounce

Bounce takes any audio loop (drums, a melody, a flipped sample), cuts it where the sound changes, and plays the pieces back on your project's tempo in the pocket you choose: dembow, drill, amapiano, boom bap and nine more, or the exact feel of your own drums. Load your melody and your drums side by side in two lanes and Bounce locks one into the other's pocket, so your melody finally sits in the same groove as your beat.

What it does

Four steps, no time-stretching

Bounce never stretches audio. It cuts the loop at each hit and fires each piece at a new moment, so drums stay as punchy as the original at any tempo.

Load

Drop your melody or sample on the MELODY lane and, if you like, your drums on the DRUMS lane (or press Load melody / Load drums). WAV, AIFF or FLAC, up to 60 seconds each.

Cut

On drum hits, on note and chord changes for melodies, or on an even beat grid for dense samples. Auto-align fixes loops that start a few ms off.

Re-groove

One lane leads and the other is locked into its pocket, or both ride a genre pocket: each slice is pulled onto the 16th grid, then moved, some hits late, some early, some louder.

Play & drag

Slices fire from FL's playhead, sample-accurate. Each lane has its own Drag out: drag it (or the lane itself) into a track for a 24-bit WAV.

New in 1.5

What changed since 1.2

Two lanes (1.5)

A MELODY lane and a DRUMS lane, each loaded, played and dragged out on its own. The Pocket switch decides who follows whom. See Two lanes & Pocket.

Per-lane Level and Clear (1.5)

Each lane has its own volume (fully down = off) and a Clear button. Both loops and the Pocket setting are saved in your FL project.

Finds the cut mode for you (1.4)

When you load a melody, Bounce listens and sets Cut on to Melodic or Drums by itself. Grid is never changed, because that is always a deliberate choice.

No lag (1.4)

Loading, re-cutting and FL's autosave no longer freeze the window, and moving a knob while a long loop is being cut drops the old job instead of queueing it. Drag out is rendered ahead while your mouse is over it, so the drag starts at once.

Tempo-aware cutting (1.4)

When FL's tempo is known, Bounce uses it to read the loop's length in bars, so samples stop playing at double or half speed.

Bigger window (1.5)

The window is 1160 × 780 to fit both lanes; drag its corner to resize.

The big one

Lock your melody to your drums

A melody or sample that "doesn't fall" with your drums is almost always one of four problems. Bounce fixes each one separately, so you can hear which it was.

A It swings differently

Your drums lean one way and the sample another, so notes land between the drum hits. Load your drums in the DRUMS lane and leave Pocket on DRUMS LEAD: Bounce learns their exact pocket and the sample's notes move to it. The drum hits appear as markers along the top of the melody lane, so you can see the notes lock under them.

B It's early or late as a whole

Many samples are cut a few milliseconds before or after the beat, or start on a pickup. Auto-align finds where the sample's beat really sits (it shows "Aligned +12 ms" on the grid). Shift slides bar 1 by whole 16ths for pickups.

C There are no hits to cut

Pads, chords and vocals change notes without a drum-like attack, so a transient detector sees nothing. Cut on: Melodic listens to which notes are sounding and cuts where the harmony changes. Cut on: Grid chops dense samples every 16th, 8th or beat, on zero crossings so nothing clicks.

D Moving notes leaves holes

Push a chord later and there's silence where it used to start. Hold keeps each note sounding, using a seamless loop of its own tail, until the next one arrives. Cuts crossfade over 8 ms. Hold is automatic for Melodic and Grid.

The 30-second lock

  1. Drop your melody or sample on the MELODY lane (or Load melody). Bounce picks Melodic or Drums cutting by itself; choose Grid for a dense flip (8TH for soul samples, 16TH for busy ones).
  2. Bounce your drum loop to audio in FL and drop it on the DRUMS lane (or Load drums).
  3. Leave Pocket on DRUMS LEAD. The drums keep their feel; the melody is locked to them, its own wobble removed first. The lane says LOCKED TO DRUMS with the swing it heard.
  4. If the downbeat still sounds off, try Shift ±1 or ±2 (pickups), and check Bars is the sample's real length.
  5. Set Accent to 0 so chord volumes stay even, then press play. If your drums already play in FL, turn the drums lane's Level fully down.
  6. Drag the melody lane's Drag out into a track: the file is named "locked to (your drums)".

Try it in the Pocket Lab below: switch it to Melody lock to hear a chord loop played straight against grooved drums, then locked to them.

Every control

What each knob does to the hits

Each diagram is one bar of 16ths: grey ticks are the loop as it came in, champagne is the result.

The pockets

13 grooves, 42 presets

Every pocket is a map of the 16 steps in a bar: how early or late each one sits, plus whether it's louder or softer. The bar under each step shows the move: up is late, down is early, and longer means further. Each preset is a different take on the pocket: more swing, tighter, pushed or laid back, or played at half or double time.

Time modality

Half-time and double-time

Half-time plays the loop's grid at half speed: a 2-bar loop now fills 4 bars of your project, every gap twice as long. Use it to turn a 140 BPM drill loop into a 70 BPM half-time slide, or reggaeton into moombahton.

Double-time plays it twice as fast: a loop's 16ths become 32nds. A hat or perc loop turns into rolls; a slow Jersey loop becomes a club run. The pocket still applies, so double-time trap hats keep their swing.

Because Bounce fires slices instead of stretching audio, each hit keeps its original pitch and punch. Half-time leaves short gaps after each hit, which is the half-time sound. Drag-out file names say which mode was used.

Two lanes & Pocket

Melody and drums, in one pocket

Bounce holds two loops. The MELODY lane is for a melody, a sample, chops or a vocal; the DRUMS lane is for drums or percussion. Each is loaded, played and dragged out on its own. With one lane loaded Bounce works exactly like before: the loop rides the pocket you pick. With both loaded, the Pocket switch decides who follows whom.

DRUMS LEAD (default)

The drums play with their own feel, untouched. Bounce measures how far every drum hit sits from the grid on each of the 16 steps and how loud each step is, and locks the melody into that: its own wobble is cleaned up first (Tighten 100 %), then the drums' timing goes on at 100 %. Triangles along the top of the melody lane show the drum hits.

MELODY LEADS

The other way round: the melody keeps its feel and the drums are locked into the melody's pocket. Good when the sample is the record and the drums were programmed straight.

GROOVE

Nobody leads: both lanes ride the genre pocket or preset you pick, with every Feel knob working. Use it to put a melody and a drum loop in a dembow or drill pocket together.

Drag-out files are named for what was done: locked to (the other loop), original feel for the lane that leads, or the groove's name. Both loops and the Pocket setting are saved inside your FL project.

Copy the feel of anything

The drums lane is also a reference slot: drop any loop you like the feel of there (a break, a percussion loop, your own finger-drummed hats bounced to audio) and turn its Level fully down. With DRUMS LEAD, your melody takes that feel without the reference being heard. Steps it never heard borrow the timing of steps with the same role (downbeats, 8ths or 16ths), so a hat pattern's swing still carries over to a loop with more hits.

The drums lane is cut with a slightly higher sensitivity than the melody, because ghost notes carry most of a groove's feel. If the swing figure on the drums lane looks odd, the reference may be read at the wrong length: try a cleaner, shorter loop (1 to 4 bars).

Tips and tricks

Things nobody tells you

Each of these follows from how Bounce actually works inside, and none is obvious from the panel.

Flip a soul sample in one move

Grid, 8TH chops, Hold on, then Boom Bap Golden Era. Every 8th of the sample swings like an MPC flip and the chords sustain through the swing, without chopping anything by hand.

Cut on Grid · 8TH · Boom Bap

Turn chords into a dembow

Melodic cutting on a pad or piano loop, Sensitivity low so each piece is a whole chord, then a Dembow preset with Hold off. The chord stabs bounce on the ch-ch with short, rhythmic gaps between them.

Melodic · Sensitivity 25 % · Hold off · Dembow

Slowed without the pitch drop

Half-time with Hold on gives a slowed, spacious version of a melody that keeps its original key, unlike slowing the sample down. Pair it with Lo-Fi Half-Time Rain for the slowed-and-reverb feel without detuning.

Half-time · Hold on · Lo-Fi

Find the real "one"

If a sample's loop point isn't its downbeat, step Shift through −4…+4 while it plays with your drums. The setting where the chord changes land on your kick is the one.

Shift while playing

Clean tempo changes, no artefacts

Pick Straight, Tighten 100, Amount 0. Bounce now only follows tempo, slice by slice, so a 90 BPM loop drops into a 140 session without smearing or pitch change, which a time-stretch can't promise.

Preset: Straight · Quantize Clean

Keep a roll in one piece

A slice carries everything until the next detected hit. Lower Sensitivity and a hat roll or flam stays glued to the hit before it and moves as one, instead of each tick being re-grooved separately.

Sensitivity 20–35 %

Swing 32nds or 8ths with Bars

Bars tells Bounce how long the loop is, and that sets the grid. Tell it a 2-bar loop is 4 bars and the "16ths" become 32nds, so the swing lands on 32nd hats. Tell it 1 bar and the swing moves to 8ths: instant boom-bap 8th shuffle.

Bars: double or half the real length

Double-tracked width

Humanize uses the same random pattern every time and scales it with the amount. Put the loop on two channels, pan them hard left and right, and set Humanize to 3 ms on one and 9 ms on the other: the hits flam differently on each side, and it sounds like a live double.

Two instances · Humanize 3 / 9 ms

Pushed hats, lazy snare

Split the loop into two copies, high-passed hats and low-passed kick/snare. Bounce both with the same pocket, then set Feel −6 ms on the hats and +8 ms on the drums. The classic "hats ahead, snare behind" tension comes from one loop.

Feel −6 / +8 ms on two instances

Put a melody in a drummer's pocket

Learn the groove from a drum break you love, then load a melody or chord loop. Every note change is a slice, so the chords now sit exactly where that drummer played. With Sensitivity low, each chord stab moves as one piece.

Learn a break → load the melody

Your own feel on every loop

Finger-drum 2 bars of hats on your pads, bounce that to audio, and Learn it. Now every loop in the beat (percs, guitar, vocal chops) can share your timing, so the whole track grooves like one player.

Record 2 bars → Learn → LEARNED on every track

Stutter gaps on vocal chops

Moved-late hits end in a 4 ms fade, so they leave clean silence instead of a click. Push Feel to +15 ms with Amount 150 % on a vocal chop loop and you get tight, rhythmic gaps that sound edited by hand.

Feel +15 ms · Amount 150 %

Make new samples to sell or flip

Drag out renders at the project tempo with the current pocket and Time mode. Change the session BPM, pocket or mode and drag again: one loop becomes a pack of tempo- and genre-matched variations, ready for Epsilon Chop.

Change BPM / preset → Drag out → repeat

Audition with FL stopped

Turn on Preview and step through presets with the arrows while FL is stopped. Set the Tempo knob to your project BPM so what you hear matches what you'll get on play.

Preview + preset arrows

Exaggerate, then flatten

Amount over 100 % pushes a pocket past the genre. Pair it with Accent 0 to move the timing without changing levels, which is ideal for melodic loops where volume jumps would sound wrong.

Amount 130–150 % · Accent 0

Fix a sloppy sample before anything else

Old vinyl breaks drift. Straight with Tighten 100 snaps them to the grid; then pick Boom Bap and the swing is applied to clean timing instead of piling on top of the drift.

Tighten 100 first, then a pocket
Under the hood

How Bounce hears and plays

Finding hits

A spectral-flux detector compares each 6 ms frame with the last and flags sudden new energy. An adaptive threshold set by Sensitivity ignores tails and reverb; hits closer than 45 ms merge.

The real attack

Each hit is traced back sample by sample to where the sound starts rising, then 3 ms of pre-roll is kept. Bounce fires each slice early by that pre-roll so the attack itself lands on the target.

Hearing notes

Melodic mode builds a 12-note map of each moment from the spectrum's peaks, with each frequency refined between FFT bins. It compares the notes in the next 120 ms with the last 120 ms; a big difference is a chord or note change. The short-window detector then pins the exact moment the new notes enter.

Hold

When a piece ends before the next one starts, its last 20–80 ms loops with a cosine crossfade into matching earlier samples, so the note keeps its pitch and tone. Holds stop after 4 seconds with a 50 ms fade.

Auto-align

The loudest hits (kicks and snares, which are rarely swung) vote on how far the loop sits from the 16th grid. Their weighted median is applied only when they agree, so a swung loop isn't mistaken for a late one.

Bar count

Auto picks the number of bars that puts the loop between 85 and 170 BPM, where trap, drill and dembow live. Override it with Bars when a loop is unusual.

Placement

For each hit: start from where it was, pull it toward the nearest 16th by Tighten, add the pocket's offset for that step times Amount, the Swing knob on off-16ths, then Feel and Humanize in milliseconds.

Playback

Slices trigger from FL's playhead with sample accuracy; tempo changes and loop jumps re-sync instantly. A new hit chokes the last with a 4 ms fade. No audio is allocated while playing.

Light

Timed on every build on GitHub's Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. Cutting, the session save and Drag out all run on background threads, so loading never glitches playback or freezes the window.

Fixes & FAQ

If something sounds off

My melody still doesn't sit with the drums

Work through the four fixes in order: drums in the DRUMS lane with Pocket on DRUMS LEAD (feel), Auto-align on and Shift (offset), Cut on Melodic or Grid (cuts), Hold on (gaps). Also check Bars: a 4-bar sample read as 2 bars will never line up.

Notes get cut off or sound choppy

Turn Hold on (or leave it on AUTO with Melodic or Grid). If cuts land mid-note, lower Sensitivity in Melodic mode, or switch to Grid with 8TH chops.

The loop plays at double or half speed

Bounce guessed the wrong number of bars. Set Bars to the loop's real length (count the kicks or check the sample's BPM).

Hits sound doubled or flammy

Sensitivity is catching a hit's own tail. Lower it until each hit is one champagne bar on the grid.

Ghost notes don't move with the groove

Raise Sensitivity so soft hits get their own slice.

Nothing plays

Bounce is an instrument: load it on a channel and press play in FL, or turn on Preview. Check that neither lane's Level is fully down. With Host sync on it follows FL's transport only.

The groove feels too much

Lower Amount first; it scales the whole pocket. Then Swing back to 0 if a preset added some.

It says "not enough hits to read a pocket"

The drums lane needs at least four clear hits to lock anything to it. Use a drum loop rather than a pad or a full mix.

I hear my drums twice

Your drums play in FL and in Bounce. Turn the drums lane's Level fully down: it still sets the pocket, it just isn't heard.

The Pocket switch or the genre chips are greyed out

Pocket only matters with both lanes loaded. The genre chips and Amount, Swing, Tighten, Humanize and Feel are dimmed while one lane leads, because the lock sets them; switch Pocket to GROOVE to use them again.

macOS says the plugin is damaged

Test builds aren't notarised yet. Run the xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine line from INSTALL.txt, then rescan plugins in FL (Options → Manage plugins → Find installed plugins).
