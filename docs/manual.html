Epsilon Bounce

Bounce takes any audio loop, finds every hit, and plays those hits back on your project's tempo in the pocket you choose: dembow, drill, amapiano, boom bap and nine more, or the exact feel of any reference loop you teach it.

What it does

Four steps, no time-stretching

Bounce never stretches audio. It cuts the loop at each hit and fires each piece at a new moment, so drums stay as punchy as the original at any tempo.

Load

Drop a WAV, AIFF or FLAC onto the grid, or press Load loop. Up to 60 seconds: drums, percs, melodies, vocal chops.

Slice

Bounce finds every hit, walks back to where its attack really starts, and keeps 3 ms before it so transients stay whole.

Re-groove

Each slice is pulled onto the 16th grid by Tighten, then moved by the pocket: some hits late, some early, some louder.

Play & drag

Slices fire from FL's playhead, sample-accurate. Drag out drops the result into a track as a 24-bit WAV.

Every control

What each knob does to the hits

Each diagram is one bar of 16ths: grey ticks are the loop as it came in, champagne is the result.

The pockets

13 grooves, 42 presets

Every pocket is a map of the 16 steps in a bar: how early or late each one sits, plus whether it's louder or softer. The bar under each step shows the move: up is late, down is early, and longer means further. Each preset is a different take on the pocket: more swing, tighter, pushed or laid back, or played at half or double time.

Time modality

Half-time and double-time

Half-time plays the loop's grid at half speed: a 2-bar loop now fills 4 bars of your project, every gap twice as long. Use it to turn a 140 BPM drill loop into a 70 BPM half-time slide, or reggaeton into moombahton.

Double-time plays it twice as fast: a loop's 16ths become 32nds. A hat or perc loop turns into rolls; a slow Jersey loop becomes a club run. The pocket still applies, so double-time trap hats keep their swing.

Because Bounce fires slices instead of stretching audio, each hit keeps its original pitch and punch. Half-time leaves short gaps after each hit, which is the half-time sound. Drag-out file names say which mode was used.

Groove Learn

Copy the feel of anything

Press Learn groove and pick a reference loop, or hold Alt / Option while dropping it on the grid. Bounce listens to it, measures how far every hit sits from the grid on each of the 16 steps, and how loud each step is. That becomes the LEARNED pocket, applied to your loop with every Feel knob still working.

Underneath the button Bounce shows what it heard: how many hits, the swing as an MPC-style percentage, and how many of the 16 steps the reference actually played. Steps it never heard borrow the timing of steps with the same role (downbeats, 8ths or 16ths), so a hat pattern's swing still carries over to a loop with more hits. The learned feel is saved inside your FL project.

Learning uses a slightly higher sensitivity than slicing, because ghost notes carry most of a groove's feel. If the reference reads at the wrong length (the swing figure looks odd), set Bars to the reference's real length before learning.

Tips and tricks

Things nobody tells you

Each of these follows from how Bounce actually works inside, and none is obvious from the panel.

Clean tempo changes, no artefacts

Pick Straight, Tighten 100, Amount 0. Bounce now only follows tempo, slice by slice, so a 90 BPM loop drops into a 140 session without smearing or pitch change, which a time-stretch can't promise.

Preset: Straight · Quantize Clean

Keep a roll in one piece

A slice carries everything until the next detected hit. Lower Sensitivity and a hat roll or flam stays glued to the hit before it and moves as one, instead of each tick being re-grooved separately.

Sensitivity 20–35 %

Swing 32nds or 8ths with Bars

Bars tells Bounce how long the loop is, and that sets the grid. Tell it a 2-bar loop is 4 bars and the "16ths" become 32nds, so the swing lands on 32nd hats. Tell it 1 bar and the swing moves to 8ths: instant boom-bap 8th shuffle.

Bars: double or half the real length

Double-tracked width

Humanize uses the same random pattern every time and scales it with the amount. Put the loop on two channels, pan them hard left and right, and set Humanize to 3 ms on one and 9 ms on the other: the hits flam differently on each side, and it sounds like a live double.

Two instances · Humanize 3 / 9 ms

Pushed hats, lazy snare

Split the loop into two copies, high-passed hats and low-passed kick/snare. Bounce both with the same pocket, then set Feel −6 ms on the hats and +8 ms on the drums. The classic "hats ahead, snare behind" tension comes from one loop.

Feel −6 / +8 ms on two instances

Put a melody in a drummer's pocket

Learn the groove from a drum break you love, then load a melody or chord loop. Every note change is a slice, so the chords now sit exactly where that drummer played. With Sensitivity low, each chord stab moves as one piece.

Learn a break → load the melody

Your own feel on every loop

Finger-drum 2 bars of hats on your pads, bounce that to audio, and Learn it. Now every loop in the beat (percs, guitar, vocal chops) can share your timing, so the whole track grooves like one player.

Record 2 bars → Learn → LEARNED on every track

Stutter gaps on vocal chops

Moved-late hits end in a 4 ms fade, so they leave clean silence instead of a click. Push Feel to +15 ms with Amount 150 % on a vocal chop loop and you get tight, rhythmic gaps that sound edited by hand.

Feel +15 ms · Amount 150 %

Make new samples to sell or flip

Drag out renders at the project tempo with the current pocket and Time mode. Change the session BPM, pocket or mode and drag again: one loop becomes a pack of tempo- and genre-matched variations, ready for Epsilon Chop.

Change BPM / preset → Drag out → repeat

Audition with FL stopped

Turn on Preview and step through presets with the arrows while FL is stopped. Set the Tempo knob to your project BPM so what you hear matches what you'll get on play.

Preview + preset arrows

Exaggerate, then flatten

Amount over 100 % pushes a pocket past the genre. Pair it with Accent 0 to move the timing without changing levels, which is ideal for melodic loops where volume jumps would sound wrong.

Amount 130–150 % · Accent 0

Fix a sloppy sample before anything else

Old vinyl breaks drift. Straight with Tighten 100 snaps them to the grid; then pick Boom Bap and the swing is applied to clean timing instead of piling on top of the drift.

Tighten 100 first, then a pocket
Under the hood

How Bounce hears and plays

Finding hits

A spectral-flux detector compares each 6 ms frame with the last and flags sudden new energy. An adaptive threshold set by Sensitivity ignores tails and reverb; hits closer than 45 ms merge.

The real attack

Each hit is traced back sample by sample to where the sound starts rising, then 3 ms of pre-roll is kept. Bounce fires each slice early by that pre-roll so the attack itself lands on the target.

Bar count

Auto picks the number of bars that puts the loop between 85 and 170 BPM, where trap, drill and dembow live. Override it with Bars when a loop is unusual.

Placement

For each hit: start from where it was, pull it toward the nearest 16th by Tighten, add the pocket's offset for that step times Amount, the Swing knob on off-16ths, then Feel and Humanize in milliseconds.

Playback

Slices trigger from FL's playhead with sample accuracy; tempo changes and loop jumps re-sync instantly. A new hit chokes the last with a 4 ms fade. No audio is allocated while playing.

Light

Measured on GitHub's Macs: about 0.12 % of one core on an Intel Mac, 0.02 % on Apple Silicon. Slicing runs on a background thread so loading never glitches playback.

Fixes & FAQ

If something sounds off

The loop plays at double or half speed

Bounce guessed the wrong number of bars. Set Bars to the loop's real length (count the kicks or check the sample's BPM).

Hits sound doubled or flammy

Sensitivity is catching a hit's own tail. Lower it until each hit is one champagne bar on the grid.

Ghost notes don't move with the groove

Raise Sensitivity so soft hits get their own slice.

Nothing plays

Bounce is an instrument: load it on a channel and press play in FL, or turn on Preview. With Host sync on it follows FL's transport only.

The groove feels too much

Lower Amount first; it scales the whole pocket. Then Swing back to 0 if a preset added some.

Learn says "not enough hits"

The reference needs at least four clear hits. Use a drum loop rather than a pad or a full mix.

macOS says the plugin is damaged

Test builds aren't notarised yet. Run the xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine line from INSTALL.txt, then rescan plugins in FL (Options → Manage plugins → Find installed plugins).
